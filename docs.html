Shopping guide

A few helpful details for browsing this storefront demo.

Shopping & returns

Browse the collection from the Shop or Products links. Search by name, filter by category, size, or starting price, and sort the products by name or price. Open Quick view for a short product description, or save an item as a favorite.

Add a size to your shopping bag to try the demo checkout. The cart uses each product’s starting price; the size choices do not represent real stock. A demo order is saved in this browser and appears in Order history after signing in with the same email address.

This sample site does not process real orders, payments, shipping, or returns.

For questions about the demo, contact KUMO.

Size guide

The size/length choices on product cards are for the demo only. This sample catalogue does not include verified garment measurements, stock, or fit guidance.

Accounts & privacy

Favorites, ratings, demo orders, and account details are stored in this browser only. Customer reviews are not included yet; ratings you leave are local to this device. Do not enter real payment details, a real password, or a reused password.

The current sample photos include straight-leg jeans, a men's casual shirt, and a denim-jacket outfit. Sizes and product details are sample descriptions only.
