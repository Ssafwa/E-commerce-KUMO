Shopping guide

A few helpful details for browsing this storefront demo.

Shopping & returns

Browse the collection from the Shop or Products links. Use the search button in the header to filter the products by name. This sample site does not process orders, payments, shipping, or returns.

For questions about the demo, contact KUMO.

Size guide

Product sizing and measurements have not been added to this sample catalogue. Please contact the store before purchasing from a real shop.

Accounts & privacy

The account page is a browser-only demonstration. Account records are stored in this browser and are not sent to a server. It is not suitable for real customer accounts; do not enter a real or reused password.
